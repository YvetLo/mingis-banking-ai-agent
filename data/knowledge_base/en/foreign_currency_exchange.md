# Foreign Currency Cash Exchange Guide

## Exchange Methods

### At a Branch Counter
- Visit any branch with your original ID
- For exchanges of NT$500,000 or more (or equivalent), we recommend reserving by phone or Online Banking at least 1 business day in advance to ensure sufficient cash is available

### Online Banking Reservation
- Log in to Online Banking → Foreign Currency Services → Cash Reservation
- Pick up your reserved cash at the designated branch as early as the next business day

## Fees & Limits

- **Fee**: Free for account holders
- **Single transaction limit**: Equivalent to NT$500,000 (larger amounts require a branch visit with advance reservation)
- **Annual cumulative limit**: Per Central Bank foreign exchange regulations, individuals are limited to USD$500,000 equivalent per year

## Required Documents

- Original ID
- For exchanges of NT$500,000 or more: a foreign exchange declaration form

## FAQ

**Q: Can I exchange foreign currency cash on weekends?**
A: Branch counter service is only available during business hours (Mon–Fri 09:00–15:30); not available on weekends or holidays.

**Q: Can I check today's exchange rate in advance?**
A: Yes, visit https://www.future-bank.com.tw/exchange-rate for live quoted rates — updated multiple times daily.

**Q: Can I exchange foreign cash back to NTD?**
A: Yes, converting back to NTD uses the bank's buy rate for that day, which may differ from your original exchange rate.
