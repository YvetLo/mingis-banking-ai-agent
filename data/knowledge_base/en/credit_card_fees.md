## Revolving Interest

If you do not pay the full balance by the due date, revolving interest will apply:
- **Annual Interest Rate**: 6.85% – 15% (determined by your credit profile)
- **Interest Calculation**: Charged from the transaction posting date until full repayment
- We recommend paying the full balance each month to avoid interest charges

## Cash Advance

- **Transaction Fee**: 3% of the advance amount, minimum NT$100
- **Interest Rate**: 15% per annum, charged from the day of the advance
- **Advance Limit**: Up to 50% of your credit limit, maximum NT$150,000
- Cash advances do not earn cashback rewards or bonus points

## Foreign Transaction Fees

- **Foreign Transaction Fee**: 1.5% of the transaction amount, listed separately from the purchase on your statement
- Applies to card purchases made abroad, and to purchases on overseas websites whether paid in a foreign currency or in NTD

### Refunds on Overseas Purchases: Amount Differences and Fees

When you cancel an order or return an item bought from an overseas website or store, two things can happen when the refund posts:

1. **The NTD refund differs from the original charge (it may be lower or higher)**: Foreign-currency transactions are converted to NTD both at purchase and at refund. The card network's exchange rate on the purchase date differs from the rate on the day the merchant processes the refund, so the converted NTD amount changes. This comes from exchange-rate movement, not an extra charge by the bank.
2. **The foreign transaction fee is not refunded**: Under our bank's policy, the 1.5% foreign transaction fee charged on the original purchase is not refunded, for all card types (Visa, Mastercard, JCB). No additional fee is charged on the refund itself.

If the difference is clearly larger than normal exchange-rate movement, or the merchant has confirmed the refund but it has not posted after 30 days, you can file a billing dispute and a customer service specialist will help.

## FAQ

**Q: How do I check my credit card's annual fee due date?**
A: Log in to Online Banking → Credit Card Management → Annual Fee Information.

**Q: The annual fee has been charged. Can I get a refund?**
A: If you meet the waiver conditions but have already been charged, apply for a refund within 30 days of the charge by contacting customer service.

**Q: What is my revolving interest rate?**
A: Your individual rate is based on your credit score. Log in to Online Banking → Credit Card Details to view your applicable rate.

**Q: I returned an item bought from an overseas website. Why is the NTD refund lower than what I paid, and will the foreign transaction fee be refunded?**
A: The purchase and the refund are each converted to NTD at the card network's rate on that day, so a rate change creates a difference. Under our bank's policy, the 1.5% foreign transaction fee on the original purchase is not refunded. If the difference looks abnormal or the refund hasn't posted after 30 days, you can file a billing dispute.
